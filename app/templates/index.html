What happened

The job didn't disappear. It multiplied.

You learned to build

Not in a bootcamp. On a Sunday, out of impatience. The thing you used to brief an agency for took you an afternoon, and something in your head permanently rearranged itself.

The lanes stopped holding

Legal, marketing, ops, engineering — those were containers for scarce skills. When the skill stops being scarce, the container stops meaning anything.

Now you can't answer the question

"So what do you do?" You have an honest answer and it takes four minutes, so you give the dishonest one that takes four seconds and feel slightly worse.

The actual point

We're early. That's lonely, and it's also an advantage.

Most hiring still runs on job titles, so it can't see you. It'll take years to fix. In the meantime the people who can see you are the other people it can't see. So put yourself somewhere findable, and when you need someone who does four jobs at once, look here first.
